ITC Limited — Valuation Methodology & Assumptions

1. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Architecture

A 5-year explicit Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) forecast was built using historical EBIT margins, adjusted for non-cash working capital changes and net capital expenditures. Terminal value was calculated using the Gordon Growth Model with a conservative terminal growth rate of 5.50% (benchmarked to long-term Indian GDP growth).

Cost of Capital (WACC) Calculation:
• Risk-free Rate (10Y G-Sec): 7.10%
• Equity Risk Premium (ERP): 6.00%
• Relevered Beta (β): 0.68
• Cost of Equity (Ke): 11.18% via CAPM
• Effective Tax Rate: 25.17% | Net Debt: Zero (Negative Net Debt)
• Resultant WACC: 11.20%

2. Comparable Companies Analysis (CCA) Multiples

Benchmarked against premier Indian FMCG peers including Hindustan Unilever, Dabur India, Marico, and Nestle India. While peer FMCG medians trade between 45x–55x P/E, ITC trades at an attractive discount due to cigarette revenue composition, offering significant upside as FMCG-Others margins expand and hotel demerger unlocks value.

3. Altman Z-Score Health Diagnosis

The calculated Altman Z-Score of 4.82 firmly places ITC in the "Safe Zone" (> 3.0), highlighting pristine working capital liquidity, high retained earnings, and near-zero solvency hazard.
